assets/sample-frame.jpg

An AI-generated image, made for this repo. The person in it is synthetic — not a
real creator, and not anyone's likeness.

Why it exists: the bundled demo video is fictional, so there are no real YouTube
stills behind it. This plate stands in as its frame.
`scripts/make-sample-thumbnails.ts` composites the landing page's sample
thumbnails over it using lib/thumbnails.ts — the same compositor the app runs on
the real stills it fetches from i.ytimg.com for an actual video.

  npx tsx scripts/make-sample-thumbnails.ts assets/sample-frame.jpg

The plate is deliberately dark on the left, which is where every overlay style
puts its text. That also makes it a good check on the adaptive scrim: it
measures 6–25 luminance under the text, so the veil stays near zero instead of
muddying an already-dark frame.
